About Us

What is the Path Finder Project (PFP)?

PFP is a unique training program designed to help young people discover their strengths, choose the right career path, and gain professional skills that match the job market.

The program is divided into three levels:
1.Self-Discovery & Soft Skills (Gamification-based training).
2.Career Path Assessment & Matching.
3.Professional Courses & Career Readiness.

Unlike traditional training, PFP uses Gamification, interactive tasks, and personalized assessments to create a fun, engaging, and practical learning experience.

Depending on your skills, you can join tracks like Marketing, Human Resources, Graphic Design, Software Development, and more based on market needs.
